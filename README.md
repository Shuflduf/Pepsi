**Pepsi Limbo** is a *fast-paced first-person* shooter, based around my favourite obsession. For legal reasons I'm not associated with the real Pepsi brand, but i don't really care.

## Includes:
- Custom-made 3D models
- 2 Tutorials
- PORTALS!!!
- Wave-based combat
- Enemies where I looked at random stuff around my house and evilified it
- Level editor
- And more!

*More info, more screenshots, and downloads available on the [Itch.io Page](https://shuflduf.itch.io/pepsi-limbo)*
